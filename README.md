An interactive educational website introducing secondary school students to three
cutting-edge fields of computer science: Large Language Models, Quantum Computing,
and Robotics. Each topic is covered through an overview, real-world applications,
and career pathways — available in both English and Greek.

## Topics

| | |
|---|---|
| Large Language Models | How LLMs work, their applications, and careers in AI |
| Quantum Computing | Principles of quantum systems and where the field is heading |
| Robotics | From sensors to autonomous systems and industry use cases |
